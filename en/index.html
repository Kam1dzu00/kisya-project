Only fresh

How it works

1. Pick a language

Tap /start and choose English or Russian — the bot and the app switch to it.

2. A two-minute profile

Specialties, remote or your city, English- and/or Russian-language jobs, minimum pay, stop words.

3. Jobs in Telegram

A digest every 1–24 hours, as many jobs at a time as you like, quiet at night. Everything else is in the app feed.

FAQ

What is Kisya Project?

A Telegram job search bot (@KisyaProject_Bot) with a mini app. It collects jobs from boards, company sites and hundreds of Telegram channels and sends only the ones that match your profile.

Is it available in English?

Yes — the bot asks for your language right after /start. You can get English-language jobs, Russian-language jobs, or both.

Does the bot apply for me?

No: it finds jobs and links to the employer; you apply yourself. An AI cover-letter assistant is in development.
